About Me

I am currently researching brain-heart coupling and specifically, how it relates to mental fatigue.

I am also interested in teleological questions in physiology, as well as connecting high level behavior to low level neural mechanisms.

I also develop games both inside and outside of research, including a recent biosignal-controlled rhythm game designed to incorporate BCI technology with a focus on smooth and intuitive controls.
